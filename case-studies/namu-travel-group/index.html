Namu Travel Group

Turning a corporate travel website into an experience worth exploring.

Namu Travel Group creates personalized travel experiences across Central America. I redesigned its digital experience to better reflect the sense of discovery and adventure behind the journeys it creates.

Role Branding · Web Design · UI/UX Design
Live site namutravel.com
Before After

Drag the handle to reveal the redesign — or focus it and press ← →

The Challenge

Namu's existing website communicated its services—but didn't fully communicate the experience.

Company information, benefits, and destinations competed for attention, making the site feel more like an informational brochure than the beginning of a journey.

The goal

Create a more immersive experience that inspires travelers to discover, explore, and imagine their next trip.

Before

After

Finding the Right Direction

Before designing the website, I explored three distinct visual directions with the client—each offering a different interpretation of premium travel.

The selected direction became the visual foundation for the final experience.

Designing for Exploration

Travel starts with curiosity.

Namu operates across multiple destinations, so I wanted discovery itself to become part of the experience.

I designed an interactive map that allows travelers to explore destinations geographically and dive deeper into each one—turning navigation into part of the journey.

The rest of the experience was designed to guide visitors from:

Discover→ Explore→ Trust→ Inquire

Before

After

The Result

A more immersive, destination-led digital experience that better reflects the personalized journeys Namu Travel creates.

17+ Pages

A full site architecture — homepage, destinations, itineraries, advisors, and press, each built from the same design system.

6 Destinations

Costa Rica, Belize, Panama, Colombia, Guatemala, and Nicaragua — each with its own page, its own photography, and its own pin on the map.

1 Interactive destination map

The centerpiece. Navigation stopped being a menu and became the thing people came to do.

  • Homepage & destination page templates
  • Interactive destination map
  • Sample itinerary pages
  • Travel advisor partner section
  • Press room & Namu Spotlight
  • Testimonial & affiliate modules
  • Newsletter & contact flows
